# Praxis: an unofficial CIPM study companion

A study companion for Claude, for experienced privacy and data protection professionals preparing for the CIPM exam. It takes the real-world program, governance and stakeholder experience you already have and helps you translate it into the reasoning the exam expects.

**Unofficial.** Praxis is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the IAPP. IAPP and its certification names belong to the IAPP. Praxis contains no IAPP textbook content and no real exam questions, and it does not give legal advice.

## What it does

- **Explain:** why the exam expects a given answer, with just enough background to follow the logic.
- **Translate:** turn a scenario from your own work into exam framework language.
- **Quiz and drill:** short questions and targeted practice on your weak spots.
- **Scenario walk-throughs and exam simulations** with a debrief.

It treats the exam as testing alignment with the framework's point of view, not real-world optimality, and it says so calmly when the two differ. It does not flatter you and it does not re-teach what you already know.

## Keep it current

The exam changes every year. Praxis carries a short map of the six exam domains from the IAPP Body of Knowledge version 4.2.0 (effective 1 September 2025), but that version may no longer be current. For the best results, download the current CIPM Body of Knowledge and Exam Blueprint from the IAPP website and attach it to your chat. Praxis will work from it. Praxis writes its own practice questions and will not reproduce real exam questions.

## Install (Claude)

1. Download `praxis-cipm-study-companion.zip` from the Releases page, or from the repository files.
2. In Claude, open **Customize > Skills** and upload the zip. Menu names vary by plan and app version. If you do not see Skills, look for **Plugins > Add > Upload plugin**.
3. Start a chat and try one of these:
   - "Why does the CIPM expect this answer?"
   - "Translate this scenario into exam logic."
   - "Give me a CIPM-style question and debrief."

Praxis is packaged as an Agent Skill, an open format that other tools also support. I have only tested it in Claude.

## Privacy

Whatever you paste goes to the AI tool you are using. Do not share confidential employer material, personal data or anything else you would not want processed there.
